How to play

📖 FictionSort by the author’s last name, A to Z. If authors match, sort by title.
🔢 Dewey DecimalSort by the number first, then the author letters.
🖐️ Move booksDrag and drop with a mouse or touch screen. Keyboard users can focus a book and press ← or →.
✅ Check your shelfGet immediate feedback, try a hint, or start a new shuffled round whenever you like.
